# Engineered Feature Documentation

The script **`fatigue_model/src/engineer_features.py`** defines **16** engineered model‑input features. The table below records the exact source columns, formula used in the code, a concise meaning, and why each feature is distinct from the others.

| Feature | Source column(s) | Formula (as in script) | Meaning / Interpretation | Why it is distinct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sleep_duration_min** | `sleep__total_asleep_minutes` | `sleep__total_asleep_minutes` (direct) | Total minutes asleep across all sleep bouts (main sleep + naps). Uses the Fitbit‑computed value that merges bouts without double‑counting. | Preferred over `sleep__asleep__sum` (Evidation) because the latter has a few anomalous rows where it exceeds in‑bed minutes. |
| **sleep_efficiency_pct** | `sleep__main_efficiency` | `sleep__main_efficiency` (direct, 0–100) | Fitbit sleep‑efficiency for the main sleep bout: `asleep / in‑bed × 100`. Indicates how much time in bed was actually spent asleep. | Captures a ratio that condenses duration + in‑bed time into a single quality metric; unlike `sleep__loop_main_asleep_frac` (Evidation) it is the official Fitbit score. |
| **sleep_fragmentation_index** | `sleep__really_awake__sum`, `sleep__total_asleep_minutes` | `sleep__really_awake__sum / (sleep__total_asleep_minutes + sleep__really_awake__sum)` | Proportion of the total sleep‑window (asleep + truly awake) spent in **full wakefulness**. Higher values = more fragmented sleep. | Differentiates from efficiency (overall quality) and from `sleep__awake__sum` (which includes restless minutes). |
| **sleep_onset_latency_min** | `sleep__loop_main_until_sleep_minutes` | `sleep__loop_main_until_sleep_minutes` (direct) | Minutes spent in bed **before** falling asleep (pre‑sleep latency). | Measures a pre‑sleep difficulty that is not captured by duration, efficiency or fragmentation. |
| **total_steps** | `steps__sum` | `steps__sum` (direct) | Total step count for the day. Primary indicator of overall daily movement volume. | Distinct from `steps__count` (non‑zero minutes) and from intensity‑based metrics. |
| **active_minutes** | `steps__light_activity__sum`, `steps__mvpa__sum` | `steps__light_activity__sum + steps__mvpa__sum` | Total minutes spent in **non‑sedentary purposeful activity** (light = 50‑100 steps/min, MVPA = >100 steps/min). | Complements `total_steps` (volume) by measuring **time** engaged in movement, regardless of intensity. |
| **mvpa_minutes** | `steps__mvpa__sum` | `steps__mvpa__sum` (direct, >100 steps/min) | Minutes of moderate‑to‑vigorous physical activity. | Isolated from `active_minutes` to capture the higher‑intensity component that has distinct physiological relevance. |
| **sedentary_minutes** | `steps__sedentary__sum` | `steps__sedentary__sum` (direct, <50 steps/min) | Minutes classified as sedentary (very low movement). | Provides a complementary “low‑activity” signal not inferable from step totals. |
| **peak_activity_intensity** | `steps__max_rolling_30` | `steps__max_rolling_30` (direct) | Maximum steps accumulated in any 30‑minute window. Captures the **single most intense bout** of activity. | Different from total/average activity; reflects peak demand that may affect same‑day recovery. |
| **resting_hr_bpm** | `heart_rate__resting_heart_rate` | `heart_rate__resting_heart_rate` (direct) | Resting heart rate (beats per minute) measured by Fitbit during the lowest‑HR period. | More clinically valid than `heart_rate__not_moving__mean`, which mixes upright still periods. |
| **hr_mean_bpm** | `heart_rate__mean` | `heart_rate__mean` (direct, all minutes with HR > 0) | Daily mean heart rate across all recorded minutes. Reflects overall cardiac load for the day. | Complements `resting_hr_bpm` (baseline) by summarising the **average** workload. |
| **hr_range_bpm** | `heart_rate__perc_95th`, `heart_rate__perc_5th` | `heart_rate__perc_95th - heart_rate__perc_5th` | Inter‑percentile range (95th – 5th) of heart rate, a robust measure of variation. | Unlike `heart_rate__stddev`, this range is less sensitive to outliers and captures total spread. |
| **hr_elevation_ratio** | `heart_rate__mean`, `heart_rate__resting_heart_rate` | `heart_rate__mean / heart_rate__resting_heart_rate` | Ratio of daily mean HR to resting HR. Indicates how much the heart worked **above baseline**. | Normalises daily load to each individual’s baseline, providing a person‑relative metric. |
| **hr_high_intensity_frac** | `heart_rate__high_150_perc_resting_frac` | `heart_rate__high_150_perc_resting_frac` (direct) | Fraction of minutes where HR > 1.5 × resting HR. Captures time spent at **high relative intensity**. | Distinct from `hr_elevation_ratio` (average) and from `hr_range_bpm` (spread); focuses on duration of high‑intensity effort. |
| **hr_temporal_consistency** | `heart_rate__autocorr10min` | `heart_rate__autocorr10min` (direct) | 10‑minute autocorrelation of HR. High values → smooth, consistent HR; low values → abrupt fluctuations. | Measures **temporal structure** of HR variation, independent of overall spread. |
| **fitbit_wear_fraction** | `active_fitbit__sum` | `active_fitbit__sum / 1440` (minutes in a day) | Fraction of the 24‑hour day that the Fitbit was worn and recording data. Values near 1.0 indicate near‑continuous wear. | Acts as a data‑quality covariate; not a physiological feature but essential for interpreting other metrics. |

*The script also defines `EXCLUDED_RAW` columns that are deliberately omitted from the engineered output; their rationale is documented in the source code.*
