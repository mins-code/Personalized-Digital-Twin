## Protocol

- Rows: 171,831 scored, non-banked attempts; 139,204 benchmark rows after requiring >=1 prior attempt.
- Features (score history + metadata): point-in-time at `deadline - 7 days`; strictly earlier submissions of other assessments only (the target attempt is always excluded).
- Split: chronological cohorts, pairwise disjoint students: train ['2013B', '2013J'], val ['2014B'], test ['2014J'].
- Seed 42; 1000 student-clustered bootstrap draws.
- Excluded: `vle.csv`, `final_result`, `mood`, `stress`, `fatigue`, `studentVle.csv`.

## Notes and caveats

- This is an OULAD benchmark only. It is not evidence of transfer to CogniSync quiz scores, and no causal or grade-guarantee claim is made.
- The 2014J test cohort is later than training and its assessment mix differs. BBB, for example, moves from many low-weight practice items in 2013 to four weighted TMAs in 2014. That distribution shift makes both history-only baselines have negative R2 on test even though their MAE is stable; the comparison is still on identical rows for every model.
- Exam deadlines are missing for 11 OULAD assessments; their deadline is imputed as the module-presentation end (the median exam submission is 20 days before that).
- The prediction instant is a fixed horizon before the deadline, so neither the target score nor the target's own submission timing is available to any feature.
- A prior score is treated as known on its submission day. Real marking takes days, so the most recent visible score may not have been released yet; this slightly favours the history features.
- Demographic fields are used only in this offline benchmark and must not be copied into CogniSync models without a separate fairness and product review.
- Per-student clustered bootstrap CIs are used because a student's attempts are not independent observations.
- Range widths are calibrated on validation errors of the train-only fit, while test predictions come from the train+val refit, so ranges are slightly conservative. Conformal coverage assumes validation and test errors look alike; 2014J is a later cohort, so the measured test coverage is the real check. Coverage holds on average over rows, not for each student.
